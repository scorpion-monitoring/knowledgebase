## Definition

An indicator is a quantitative or qualitative piece of evidence that documents, monitors or measures a component of an impact pathway and supports decisions for a defined audience.

## Explanation

This definition is a composite. It combines the wording of the ELIXIR Impact Toolkit, which describes indicators as evidence for the components of an impact pathway (activity, outcome and impact), with its use in monitoring. No single source gives it in these words.

Indicators may be supplemented by other kinds of evidence, such as narratives, case studies or lists of examples. An indicator is not the number itself. It names what is assessed, for example "citation impact". The <Term id="metric" /> states how it is quantified. The <Term id="measurement" /> states where the data come from. The overview "Indicator, KPI, metric, measurement: how they relate" shows the three together.

Indicators can be classified in several ways. They can be <Term id="quantitative-and-qualitative-indicators" />. They can be grouped by their place on the <Term id="results-chain" />, or by what they observe about a service. See <Term id="indicator-classes" />.

Literature on key performance indicator management finds no clear consensus on the elements that formalise an indicator, and different works use different terms for the same concept. The KB therefore states its choices on each term page.

An indicator is called a <Term id="key-performance-indicator" /> when an organisation selects it as critical to its success.

## Example

A research infrastructure wants to document the reach of a database. It chooses "number of scientific users" as an indicator. This indicator describes an activity of the infrastructure. The metric is the number of unique users per year. The measurement is the analysis of access logs.

## Not to be confused with

- <Term id="metric" />: how the indicator is quantified.
- <Term id="measurement" />: the source and method for the data.
- <Term id="observation" />: one recorded value of an indicator for a period.
- <Term id="key-performance-indicator" />: an indicator that is selected as critical. The difference is one of selection and purpose, not of kind.

- [Domínguez et al.: A taxonomy for key performance indicators management. Computer Standards & Interfaces 64 (2019) 24-40](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csi.2018.12.001) – notes the lack of consensus on the elements that formalise a performance indicator. An accepted manuscript is available at [Zaguán](https://zaguan.unizar.es/record/86434).
- [Research Infrastructure Metrics Ontology (RIMO), version 0.2.0](https://github.com/JKoblitz/BH25-metrics), Koblitz and the BioHackathon Europe 2025 METRICS Team, 2025 – describes indicators (class `KPI`) with a name, description, example, means of measurement and a flag for qualitative indicators. It has no separate class for indicators that are not KPIs.
